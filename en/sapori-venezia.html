Rialto Market

Campo de la Pescaria / Erbaria

Fish and vegetable stalls introduce the lagoon. Look for seasonal produce; head to nearby bacari for prepared food.

Information and source · Directions

Before the first bite

Ask staff about ingredients and allergens: recipes, fillings and shared frying oil vary by venue. Seasons matter for seafood, vegetables and festive sweets. Clarify price, weight and table service before ordering.
